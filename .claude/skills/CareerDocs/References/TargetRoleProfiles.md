# Target Role Profile Library

Condensed from the uploaded "2025 Ideal JDs and KPIs" reference document. These are
**exemplar roles the candidate is aiming at** (Avalara / Vertex indirect-tax, ERP-partner,
and e-Invoicing ecosystem roles) — not live applications. Use this file to recognize
the terminology, KPI language, and seniority signals these companies favor, and to spot
when a newly pasted JD belongs to this same role family (helps keyword analysis move
faster and flags near-duplicate applications even when exact text differs).

Do not log these as processed JDs in `Data/JDLog.json` — they are background reference,
not applications the candidate has submitted or is submitting against right now.

## 1. Avalara — US Enterprise CAM (Customer Account Manager)
- Owns net revenue growth for a 20-30 Enterprise account portfolio via upsell/add-on.
- Full renewal sales cycle ownership, quota-carrying, forecasting, risk analysis via Salesforce.
- Consultative selling: uncover sales-and-use-tax challenges, recommend product fit.
- Win-win renewal negotiation; cross-departmental partnering for customer success.
- Required: 5+ years sales/customer management/consulting; executive-level comms;
  contract negotiation; multitasking under duress.

## 2. Avalara — EMEA Senior CAM
- Post-sale primary contact; drives adoption/utilization via product + technical expertise.
- Owns upsell/cross-sell revenue and renewal/retention results.
- VAT / Sales & Use Tax accounting-concept fluency is a strong plus.
- Required: 10+ years; enterprise account experience; calm-demeanor teamwork;
  investigative programming for self-directed problem solving; CRM proficiency;
  multilingual a plus (second European language desirable).

## 3. Avalara — US CAM Manager
- Leads/recruits/trains/mentors a team of 5-10 Account Managers.
- Owns team forecasting, pipeline, and a structured rhythm-of-business cadence.
- Growth + Retention + Satisfaction triad: compliance-aligned upsell, at-risk-renewal
  rescue, CRM-documented escalation resolution.
- Required: 10+ years sales/professional-services/account-management leadership;
  hands-on coaching and positive reinforcement; cloud-based software experience
  (account management, pre-sales, solutions consulting, renewals, TAM).

## 4. Vertex — SAP Channel Manager (Business Development Executive)
- Individual-contributor, quota-carrying co-sell lead with SAP as strategic partner.
- Lead gen through SAP Global Account Directors; RFP execution as pre-sales SME.
- Requires strong SAP knowledge, SAP S/4HANA integration understanding, O2C/P2P and
  GL/transaction-tax-flow familiarity.
- Requires French and English fluency (explicit requirement).
- Education: Bachelor's + 4 years selling SAP software or indirect SAP-ecosystem
  selling; SAP S/4HANA knowledge recommended; MBA/CPA preferred.

## 5. Avalara — Director, Strategic Enterprise Partners
- Sets global multi-year GTM strategy across the enterprise partner ecosystem
  (ERP publishers, ISVs, systems integrators — especially Oracle ecosystem).
- Builds a rolling 12-month joint product development roadmap with key partners.
- Data-driven business case / trade-off analysis to guide ARR-growth decisions.
- Required: Bachelor's + 15 years strategy consulting or GTM business development;
  10+ years managing strategic ERP-ecosystem partnerships; predictive modeling / BI
  experience; proof of measurable partner-influenced revenue impact.
- Company is explicitly "AI-first" — candidates expected to apply AI daily to
  forecasting, partner scoring, and workflow automation.
- 2025 KPI families: Partner-Influenced ARR, Partner-Sourced Pipeline, Partner-Attach
  Rate, Joint Product Roadmap Delivery Rate (≥80% on-time norm), AI-based partner
  scoring adoption, Executive Relationship Strength Index.

## 6. Vertex — Consulting / Professional Services Sales (E-Invoicing PM track)
- Sells consulting/professional services at expert level; quota-carrying.
- Owns Statement of Work (SOW) delivery, RACI diagrams, Gantt scheduling for
  implementation strategy; negotiates and redlines SOWs/Master Agreements with Legal.
- Expert-level O2C/P2P and ERP/financial-systems understanding.
- Required: Bachelor's + 10 years selling tax solutions or enterprise software and
  professional services; ERP experience across SAP, Oracle, JD Edwards, Microsoft.
- Vertex "Winning Way" behaviors to mirror in tone: Communicate with Clarity, Act with
  Urgency, Work with Purpose, Drive to Decision, Own the Outcome.
- 2025 KPI families: Services Revenue Attainment (≥100% target), Pipeline Coverage
  Ratio (3-5x quarterly quota), SOW Accuracy/Rework Rate (<10% healthy), AI-Enhanced
  Forecasting Usage, Automation Utilization Rate.

## 7. Vertex — Senior Global e-Invoicing Leadership (GPS)
- Senior leader owning global e-Invoicing strategy across Customer Success/Services.
- Enterprise SME on CTC (Continuous Transaction Controls), PEPPOL, EDI, and
  country-specific e-Invoicing compliance frameworks.
- Owns GPS e-Invoicing pipeline, bookings, revenue targets, and capacity planning.
- Executive sponsor on major implementations; builds partner/regulator relationships;
  drives regulatory-change forecasting into the product roadmap.
- Required: 10+ years in e-Invoicing/EDI domain leading global compliance initiatives;
  15+ years in consulting/client-services leadership; large-scale complex
  implementation management; Europe location preference; international travel.
- 2025 KPI families: Market Thought Leadership Index, Regulatory Intelligence Impact
  Score, e-Invoicing Bookings Attainment, Software Adoption Rate, Implementation
  Success Rate, AI-enabled compliance prediction adoption, AI-assisted SOW creation
  usage.

## Cross-role terminology cheat sheet

Recurring terms across this role family — treat these as near-universal keywords to
expect in any new JD in this space, and check them first during keyword analysis:

Quota, pipeline, forecasting, renewal sales cycle, upsell, cross-sell, account
expansion, consultative/enterprise solution selling, co-selling, RFP, SOW, RACI, Gantt,
contract redlining, Order-to-Cash, Procure-to-Pay, indirect tax (VAT / Sales & Use Tax),
e-Invoicing (CTC, PEPPOL, EDI), SAP / SAP S/4HANA, Oracle Cloud / JD Edwards, Salesforce
CRM, executive-level relationships, trusted advisor, calm demeanor under duress,
self-starter / natural curiosity, investigative programming, AI-enhanced forecasting /
AI-assisted SOW creation / AI-enabled compliance monitoring, English/French fluency.
