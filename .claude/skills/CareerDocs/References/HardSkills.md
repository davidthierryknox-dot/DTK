## Convergent Action, Structuring Chaos & Divergent Thinking (Hard-Skill Framing)

These three traits are classified as HARD skills because each is an operational,
teachable, testable methodology — not a disposition. Use this framing verbatim when a
JD signals ambiguity-tolerance, 0-to-1 building, or "figure it out as you go" work
(see `References/ExperienceOverSkills.md`).

- **Convergent Action** — The functional capability to execute, build, and translate
  conceptual models into concrete products, operational systems, and measurable
  deliverables (e.g., shipping a product, scoping an SOW, establishing a commercial
  framework).
- **Structuring Chaos** — Technical systems design and workflow architecture: defining
  RACI models, building operating rhythms, drafting governance policies, and organizing
  resources into repeatable pipelines.
- **Divergent Thinking** — A structured cognitive/analytical methodology: synthesizing
  disparate datasets, cross-mapping technical architectures, and identifying functional
  edge cases across non-linear domains.
